# meeting-karigor-mcp

**Using Meeting Karigor from your AI assistant.** For people whose organization uses Meeting Karigor, and whose AI assistant (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Codex) is connected to Karigor's MCP server, or will be: it teaches the assistant how Meeting Karigor really works today. Before a meeting, the assistant plans it with you as a brief whose agenda becomes items people tick in the live view; you start and record the meeting in the app. After the meeting, the assistant reads its record (and the transcript, if your organization's owner turned that tool on) and gives you the decisions, the action items with their owners, the open questions and, when you ask, a follow-up draft that you send yourself. It calls only the tools in its own tool list, checks your access first with `myAccess`, tells you, when a tool is missing, whether you only need to reconnect or which switch is off and who turns it on, from Karigor's own answer, never changes access settings, and never records, runs, sends, ticks or approves anything by itself. Recording, uploading, `Run`, ticking items, `Minutes PDF` and `Send minutes` stay in the app, and so does Meeting Karigor's own analysis. **What you need first:** a Karigor account in an organization that has Meeting Karigor. For tool access, your organization's owner turns MCP and each tool on in the Karigor app at Settings, MCP access, and a Karigor super admin turns Meeting Karigor on for MCP in your organization and gives you MCP access to it. **To start,** open `setup-prompt.md` and paste its prompt into your AI right after you accept your Karigor invite: your AI then walks you through connecting Karigor and installing this skill, and checks what you can use. Without a connection, the skill still works as a step-by-step guide to the app. **In Claude**, add the folder as a skill (Claude Code: copy it to `~/.claude/skills/meeting-karigor-mcp/`; Claude apps: zip the folder and upload it at Customize, Skills). **In ChatGPT**, connect Karigor with `setup-prompt.md`, then paste the box from `chatgpt-instructions.md` as the first message of a chat where you picked Karigor with @. A Custom GPT, a Project or a Gem with that box guides you through the app, but it does not reach Karigor's tools. **In Codex** and other tools that read the Agent Skills format, copy the folder to `~/.agents/skills/` or to `.agents/skills/` in your project. `examples.md` is its test suite: nineteen cases, each with "Must" and "Must not" lines.
