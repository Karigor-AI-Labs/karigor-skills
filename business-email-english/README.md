# business-email-english

**Professional business email in English.** For Bangladeshi professionals, founders, merchandisers, accounts and sales staff who write work email in English to local or foreign clients, suppliers and partners: it writes, or fixes, enquiries, follow-ups, quotation cover notes, payment reminders, apologies and meeting requests that are clear, warm and specific, replaces habits that confuse foreign readers ("kindly do the needful", "revert back", "PFA"), handles BDT, lakh, GMT+6, the Friday weekend and corporate payment paperwork, and always ends with one clear next step; you can ask in Bangla or Banglish and still get the email in English. **In Claude**, add the folder as a skill (Claude Code: copy it to `~/.claude/skills/business-email-english/`; Claude apps: upload the folder as a ZIP under Skills in settings), then say what email you need or paste your draft. **In ChatGPT**, follow `chatgpt-instructions.md` to make a Custom GPT or Project, or paste its box into a new chat. **In Codex** and other tools that read the Agent Skills format, copy the folder to `~/.agents/skills/` or to `.agents/skills/` in your project. `examples.md` is its test suite: eleven cases, each with "Must" and "Must not" lines.
