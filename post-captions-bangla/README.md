# post-captions-bangla

**Post captions in Bangla.** For Bangladeshi shop owners and small marketing teams who post for their business: it writes captions in Bangla or English for product posts, offers and occasions (Eid, Puja, Pohela Boishakh, Victory Day and more), for wherever the post goes, such as Facebook or Instagram, with a strong first line, the price and how to order, one clear call to action and a few relevant hashtags, and it refuses fake urgency, whitening and health claims and sales posts on days of mourning. **In Claude**, add the folder as a skill (Claude Code: copy it to `~/.claude/skills/post-captions-bangla/`; Claude apps: upload the folder as a ZIP under Skills in settings), then describe your post in a line or two. **In ChatGPT**, follow `chatgpt-instructions.md` to make a Custom GPT or Project, or paste its box into a new chat. **In Codex** and other tools that read the Agent Skills format, copy the folder to `~/.agents/skills/` or to `.agents/skills/` in your project. `examples.md` is its test suite: eleven cases, each with "Must" and "Must not" lines.
