# customer-replies-bangla

**Customer replies for online shops, in Bangla.** For Bangladeshi online sellers who answer customers by message, and for anyone building a reply assistant for an online shop: it writes short, polite replies in natural Bangla (or English, or Banglish when the customer writes that way) for price, delivery, stock, sizes, cash on delivery and bKash, order taking and polite refusals, wherever the customer writes (Facebook Messenger and comments, WhatsApp, Instagram), and it never invents a price or stock level, because it works only from a shop table the seller fills once. **In Claude**, add the folder as a skill (Claude Code: copy it to `~/.claude/skills/customer-replies-bangla/`; Claude apps: upload the folder as a ZIP under Skills in settings), then paste a customer message. **In ChatGPT**, follow `chatgpt-instructions.md` to make a Custom GPT or Project, or paste its box into a new chat. **In Codex** and other tools that read the Agent Skills format, copy the folder to `~/.agents/skills/` or to `.agents/skills/` in your project. `examples.md` is its test suite: eleven cases, each with "Must" and "Must not" lines.
